# InBTween

**InBTween** is a guided dialogue application designed to help two or more people discuss meaningful or difficult topics by introducing intentional third-person perspectives (“personas”) between them.

It creates a structured space *between* individuals and the topic at hand, reducing defensiveness while increasing clarity, accountability, and understanding.

---

## What InBTween Is

- A **conversation scaffolding tool**
- A **perspective-mapping framework**
- A **structured dialogue ritual**
- A **local, private, human-driven experience**

InBTween externalizes topics and viewpoints so participants can speak honestly without immediately collapsing into conflict or self-protection.

---

## What InBTween Is Not

- Not therapy
- Not AI counseling
- Not a chat app
- Not a conflict “solver”

InBTween does not automate insight. It creates the conditions where insight can occur.

---

## Core Concepts

### Participants
Real people who are present for the conversation.

### Personas
Intentional third-person perspectives defined by participants.  
Personas speak *about* a participant or *for* a concern the participant may struggle to express directly.

### Topic
A single word or short phrase placed at the center of the session.  
The topic is externalized and remains fixed throughout the dialogue.

### Dialogue Phases
Conversations progress through enforced phases:
1. Introduction
2. Persona Dialogue
3. Clarification
4. Direct (First-Person) Conversation

---

## Design Principles

- Psychological safety over speed
- Structure over spontaneity
- Reflection over reaction
- Accountability without judgment

---

## Ethical Use

InBTween is designed for **voluntary, good-faith participation**.  
It should not be used to coerce, interrogate, or manipulate another person.
